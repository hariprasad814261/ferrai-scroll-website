DESIGN

EVERY CONTOUR APPROVED BY IL COMMENDATORE.

01 / 04

TWIN POP-UP HEADLAMPS

Aerodynamic pop-up assemblies paired with Lexan auxiliary driving lenses. Engineered to slice through night air at 324 km/h with analog conviction.

02 / 04

SPEEDLINE MODULAR WHEELS

Forged three-piece lightweight alloy wheels with racing centre-lock nuts. 17-inch lightweight architecture shod in custom 335-section Pirelli P-Zero compound.

03 / 04

KEVLAR & NOMEX MONOCOQUE

Eleven composite body panels crafted from aeronautical carbon and Kevlar. No carpets, no radio, pull-wire door releases. 1,100 kg dry weight with zero compromise.

04 / 04

LOUVERED SCREEN & REAR WING

Sculpted fixed wing paired with a vented Lexan rear screen that showcases the twin-turbocharged 2.9L V8 while shedding heat under high boost.
